Overview

FoxNXT is looking for talent.

FoxNXT is opening a call for original animated shorts. If you've got a strong original idea, we want to help you get it made — and get it seen. Don't have your own IP to develop? You can use one of the characters from our style reference gallery as the storyline for your submission.

1

Original Animated Short

Submit an original animated short (and supporting creative docs) based on a character, world, or story that is fully yours — or build your short around a character from our reference gallery if you don't have a project of your own ready.

2

Animation Showcase

5–7 selected films screen live at Back on the Lot: Animation Edition.

3

Short Development

Three films from the screening are chosen to receive $5,000 to further develop their short.

4

Pitch Your Concept

After development, our judges review the final versions and choose a winner. The winner gets lunch with Head of FoxNXT, Jon Flynn, and a pitch with Fox Entertainment CEO, Rob Wade.

Eligibility & requirements

  • By submitting, you are agreeing that the IP is wholly original and fully owned by you, or is a character from our reference gallery.
  • Submission package must include your animated short, a pitch deck, visual style references, and character sheets.
  • Any animation style or medium is welcome — 2D, 3D, stop-motion, mixed media, and beyond.
  • 3 minute max runtime.
  • No vertical content will be accepted.
  • One submission per creator or team.
  • All materials must be submitted through the form below by the deadline. Late or incomplete submissions will not be reviewed.
